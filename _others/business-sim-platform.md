  <h3>About Situational Simulation 背景介绍</h3>
  <p>Words and patterns learned in class often stay "recognized" but never "used". Situational simulation puts language back where it belongs: you get a business scenario, a clear role and a problem your team must solve. Driven by a goal, you take a stance, make decisions and persuade colleagues in English.<br><span class="bs-note">课堂上学到的商务词汇和句型,常常停留在"认识",很难在真实交流中"用出来"。情境化模拟学习把语言放回使用场景:你拿到一个商业情境、一个明确的角色和一个需要团队解决的问题,在目标驱动下选择立场、做出决策,并用英语说服同事。</span></p>
  <div class="bs-cols">
    <div class="bs-box"><h4>Role-driven 角色驱动</h4><p>Each role has its own stance, concerns and resources, so views naturally clash, just like in real teamwork.<br><span class="bs-note">每个角色立场、关注点和资源不同,团队合作中自然出现观点碰撞。</span></p></div>
    <div class="bs-box"><h4>Goal-driven 目标驱动</h4><p>Every scenario has a problem to solve and a consensus to reach. Language serves the task.<br><span class="bs-note">每个情境都有要解决的问题和需要达成的共识,语言服务于任务。</span></p></div>
    <div class="bs-box"><h4>Language scaffolds 词句支架</h4><p>Pick keywords for stance, decision and reason first, then turn them into full sentences.<br><span class="bs-note">先选表态、决策、理由的英文词,再把词变成完整的话,降低开口门槛。</span></p></div>
    <div class="bs-box"><h4>AI support &amp; review AI 辅助与复盘</h4><p>AI expands your keywords into a full speech and lists the phrases and patterns used. Download them for revision.<br><span class="bs-note">AI 把关键词扩展为完整发言,列出用到的关键词和句式(中英双语),可下载复习。</span></p></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="bs-section">
  <h3>How It Works in Class 课堂介绍</h3>
  <div class="bs-steps">
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Read the scenario 读情境</h4><p>Read the company background, the team's problem and the role division.<br><span class="bs-note">阅读公司背景、团队要解决的问题和角色分工。</span></p></div>
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Choose a role 选角色</h4><p>Each student opens the page alone and picks one role card. In class, work in groups of three with different roles.<br><span class="bs-note">每位同学独立打开页面选一张角色卡;课堂上三人一组,各选不同角色。</span></p></div>
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Pick keywords 选关键词</h4><p>Choose English words to show your attitude, make a decision and give reasons.<br><span class="bs-note">从词库中选择表明态度、做出决策、陈述理由的英文词。</span></p></div>
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Generate your speech 生成话术</h4><p>Click "Generate" to get a full English speech and bilingual study notes. AI colleagues reply over 4 rounds.<br><span class="bs-note">点击"生成沟通话术",获得完整发言和双语学习资料;AI 同事回应,共 4 轮。</span></p></div>
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Discuss &amp; download 讨论与下载</h4><p>Hold the real discussion in your group with your own words, then download all speeches and notes.<br><span class="bs-note">小组内用自己的话真实讨论,最后下载全部话术与学习资料。</span></p></div>
  </div>
