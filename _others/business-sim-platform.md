<div class="bs-section">
  <h3>What is it? 这是什么</h3>
  <p>A business scene and a role from the unit.<br><span class="bs-note">一个单元里的商务场景和一个角色。</span></p>
  <p>Media: choose keywords, and AI turns them into a speech for the meeting.<br><span class="bs-note">媒体单元:选关键词,AI 生成会议上的发言。</span></p>
  <p>Innovation: say the line yourself. The AI coach comments on your logic and wording, then asks you to continue. After that, close the page and talk with classmates.<br><span class="bs-note">创新单元:自己把句子说出来。AI 助手点评逻辑和用词,并请你继续说。练完后离开网页,和同学当面讨论。</span></p>
</div>

<div class="bs-section">
  <h3>How to play 怎么玩</h3>
  <div class="bs-steps">
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Read the scene 读情境</h4></div>
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Pick a role 选角色</h4></div>
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Practice with the AI coach 和 AI 练自己的发言</h4></div>
    <div class="bs-box bs-step"><h4>Discuss offline 带着自己的句子和同学说</h4></div>
  </div>
  <p class="bs-note">Use your own words. The coach does not write the speech for you. 用自己的话。助手不代写整段发言。Media 单元仍是选关键词后生成话术。</p>
